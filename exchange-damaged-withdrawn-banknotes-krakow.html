Questions about damaged and withdrawn banknotes

Can every damaged banknote be exchanged?

No. It depends on the currency and on how badly the note is damaged. That is why we ask for a photo first: you get the answer before you come.

Do you buy banknotes withdrawn from circulation?

Yes, in the currencies listed above, case by case. Send a photo and we confirm the specific note and the rate.

Is the rate the same as for regular banknotes?

The rate for damaged and withdrawn banknotes is set individually and given to you before your visit.
